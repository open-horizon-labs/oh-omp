**Core tension / why this is hard:**
Two laws of different character must merge into one expert system without either corrupting the other. Chapter A is a compiled palimpsest of user decisions with explicit supersession chains and dissent-calibrated claims — fragile to re-derivation, so it must be carried verbatim-in-substance. Chapter B is distributed across living repo files (AGENTS.md, .omp/rules, ADRs) that change through normal development — so the expert system must CITE and defer to them rather than fork their content, or it becomes a stale shadow copy whose drift silently bifurcates the law (U-N6). Additionally, the expert system now governs the very protocol (delegation) under which its own maintenance tasks run — self-application means frame errors propagate into every future task AND into the gradation dataset's ground truth.

**Specific need:**
A revised frame precise enough for the builder to compile a two-chapter expert system: Chapter A as carried routing/delegation law (matrix seed = A1–A18), Chapter B as cited implementation law (matrix seed = B1–B22, citation-first to avoid shadow-copy drift), unified stop conditions, an unbound Model Roster awaiting the binding step, persistence and update rules — at the user-confirmed committed path.

**Ecosystem context, not scope:** other ai-omnibus repos and orchestrators consuming the protocol contracts (they motivate B2 but are not modifiable); upstream open-horizon-labs/oh-omp (sync source only); the workstream-subagents pipeline internals.

**Constraints:**
(1) Builder-only invocation: no code, no PRs, no product edits, no expert-system drafting in this step. (2) Chapter A carried undiluted — rescoping widens, never dilutes (user instruction). (3) Five superego advisories applied (done: advisory 1 in Aim, 2 in boundary, 3 as A18 BLOCK IF + S19, 4 in A17, 5 in Existing-Expert-System + Execution Contract). (4) No invented decisions — genuine gaps carried as material uncertainty. (5) Interactive checkpoints preserved: frame checkpoint, binding step, durable-law delta. (6) All Chapter B criteria source-cited from inspected files.

**Underspecification / uncertainties:** see Material uncertainty list under Verification / Learning. Material-and-blocking for the BUILD step: none — but U-N1 (per-package law scope) and U-N2 (rule-source precedence) must be presented at the durable-law delta for user decision, not silently resolved by the builder.

**Failure modes:**
(a) Diluting or dropping a Chapter A criterion during the merge (forbidden — carry check is a verification gate). (b) Forking AGENTS.md content into the expert system verbatim, creating a drifting shadow law. (c) Inventing per-package applicability rules (U-N1) or precedence rules (U-N2) without user decision. (d) Treating the expert system's governance of routing as authorization to implement routing (A1 still gates: governance now, implementation after ADR). (e) Restoring pre-dissent claim strength while restructuring. (f) Scope creep into other repos or interactive routing. (g) Over-narrowing: dropping dogfooding governance or the universal categorical-dispatch reading of A6. (h) Committing the artifact without per-invocation user instruction (B14 vs persistence-decision confusion).

### Solution Space

**Candidate approaches:**
- Two separate expert systems (routing + project) — rejected by user correction: one generic `oh-omp` system with routing as a chapter.
